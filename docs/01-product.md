# Product

## Goals

- Increase direct online orders.
- Improve kitchen clarity and speed.
- Preserve brand identity.
- Support future delivery and multi-location growth.
- Keep the stack maintainable for a small team.

## MVP scope

- Menu browsing
- Product customization
- Pickup-only ordering
- Secure payment
- Realtime order tracking
- Kitchen accept / delay / ready / complete
- Admin menu and hours management
- Receipt printing
- Push or SMS notifications

## Out of scope for MVP

- Delivery dispatch
- Loyalty
- Reservations
- Gift cards
- Inventory automation
- Franchise support

## UX principles

- Fewer taps
- Clear price visibility
- Large touch targets
- Strong visual hierarchy
- No clutter
- No refresh-based workflows
